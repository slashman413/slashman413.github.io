## What We Learned



### 1. AI Amplifies, Does Not Replace

AI made us 10x more productive, but human judgment was critical for product decisions, quality control, and marketing strategy.



### 2. Infrastructure > Individual Products

The most valuable asset is not any single product - it is the system that produces them: Cowork Pro, Hugo, GitHub Actions, Mautic.



### 3. Trust Is Everything

In the AI space, trust is harder to earn but more valuable than ever. We built it through honest reviews, real data, and transparent processes.



### 4. Start Small, Think Big

We did not try to build 10 products at once. We built one, validated demand, reinvested in infrastructure, then built more.
