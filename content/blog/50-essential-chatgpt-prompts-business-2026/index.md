## Part 1: Content Creation (10 Prompts)

### 1. Blog Post Outline Generator

```
Act as a professional content strategist. I run a [business type] business in the [industry] industry. Create a comprehensive blog post outline about [topic] that:

- Targets the primary keyword "[primary keyword]"
- Includes at least 6 H2 sections with 3-4 H3 subsections each
- Addresses common reader questions and objections
- Includes suggestions for data points or statistics to cite
- Ends with a compelling conclusion and call-to-action

Format the output as a structured outline with brief descriptions under each section.
```

```

4. Recommends content format for each piece (guide, listicle, case study, comparison)

```

4. **Combine prompts.** Use the output from one prompt as input for another. For example, use a market research prompt to inform your content strategy prompt.
