# How to Automate Your Weekly Report with AI (No Code)

## The Problem with Weekly Reports

Every Friday afternoon, managers and solopreneurs face the same tedious task: gathering data from multiple sources, formatting it, and writing a cohesive weekly report. Whether you're pulling metrics from Google Analytics, extracting sales data from a CRM, or summarizing team updates from Slack, compiling this information manually is a drain on productivity.

Fortunately, in 2026, you can completely **automate your weekly report with AI** using no-code tools. 

## Step-by-Step: Building an AI Reporting Workflow

To eliminate this manual work, we can build a multi-agent workflow that aggregates data and drafts the report for you.

### 1. Data Aggregation (The Trigger)
Instead of manually checking dashboards, set up a scheduled trigger (e.g., every Friday at 3 PM) in a no-code platform like n8n or Make.
- Connect your nodes to your data sources: Stripe for revenue, Google Analytics for traffic, and Feishu or Slack for team updates.
- The automation will pull the raw data automatically.

### 2. AI Summarization (The Brain)
Raw data is hard to read. This is where AI shines.
- Pass the aggregated JSON data into an AI node (like OpenAI's GPT-4 or Anthropic's Claude).
- Give the AI a specific prompt: *"You are a data analyst. Analyze the following raw metrics and team updates. Draft a concise, professional weekly report highlighting key wins, metric changes, and areas of concern."*

*Tip: Need help crafting the perfect instructions for your AI? Check out our [AI Prompt Library Usage Guide](/blog/ai-prompt-library-usage-guide-2026/) for proven templates.*

### 3. Delivery (The Action)
Once the AI generates the text, route the output to your preferred platform.
- Have the workflow email the report directly to your stakeholders.
- Or, send a draft to a Notion page or Feishu document for your final review before publishing. *(Read our [Feishu vs Notion comparison](/blog/feishu-vs-notion-comparison-2026/) to decide which is best for your team).*
