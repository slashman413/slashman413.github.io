# 內部連結策略

## 連結結構
```
Blog Articles (71)
  ├── Product Landing Pages (10)
  ├── Comparison Articles (5)
  └── How-to Guides (20)

Product Landing Pages (10)
  ├── Use Case Articles (30)
  ├── FAQ Pages (10)

  ├── Cowork Pro vs [Competitor]
  ├── AI Prompt Library vs [Competitor]
```

2. 產品頁添加「使用案例」文章
3. 創建 5 篇比較文章
4. 設置麵包屑導覽
