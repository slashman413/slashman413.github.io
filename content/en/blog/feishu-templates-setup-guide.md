# The Ultimate Feishu Templates Setup Guide

Feishu (also known as Lark globally) is a powerhouse of productivity, combining chat, document collaboration, project management, and video conferencing into a single unified platform. However, starting with a blank canvas can be overwhelming. 

To truly unlock the platform's potential, you need structured, repeatable processes. This guide will walk you through the ultimate Feishu templates setup to streamline your workspace and boost team efficiency.

## Why You Need Standardized Feishu Templates

Without templates, teams waste hours formatting documents, setting up project trackers, and structuring meeting notes. Standardized templates offer:
- **Consistency**: Every project looks and feels the same, making it easy for anyone to jump in.
- **Speed**: Start working on the actual content immediately instead of building the structure.
- **Onboarding**: New hires can understand your processes simply by looking at the templates.

## Core Templates Every Team Needs

### 1. The Daily Stand-up & Meeting Notes Template
Stop losing track of action items. A good meeting template should include:
- Date and Attendees
- Agenda items
- Discussion summary
- Action items (assigned with @mentions and due dates)

### 2. Project Tracking Bitable
Feishu's Bitable is a powerful database tool. Your project tracking template should have views for Kanban boards, Gantt charts, and standard grids. Essential columns include Task Name, Assignee, Status, Priority, and Timeline.

### 3. OKR (Objectives and Key Results) Tracker
Align your team's goals with a transparent OKR template. Use a hierarchical structure where departmental OKRs roll up into company-wide objectives.

### 4. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
Documenting how things are done is critical for scaling. An SOP template should have a clear purpose statement, a step-by-step guide with screenshots, and a FAQ section.

## How to Implement and Enforce Template Usage

### Centralize Your Templates
Create a dedicated "Template Library" folder in your Feishu Docs. Make this folder accessible to everyone but restrict editing permissions to workspace admins to prevent accidental changes.

### Use the "Save as Template" Feature
Feishu allows you to save any doc or Bitable as a custom template. Once saved, team members can select it directly from the "New" menu, eliminating the need to copy and paste old documents.

### Train Your Team
A template is only useful if people use it. Conduct a brief training session showing the team where the templates live and how to use them.
