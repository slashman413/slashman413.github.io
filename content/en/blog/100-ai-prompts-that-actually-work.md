## Why Most AI Prompts Fail

When beginners use tools like ChatGPT or Claude, they tend to treat them like search engines. A prompt like "Write a blog post about marketing" is too vague. The AI has to guess the tone, the target audience, the format, and the specific focus. 

Effective prompt engineering requires:
1. **Context**: Who are you, and what are you trying to achieve?
2. **Task**: What exactly do you want the AI to do?
3. **Format**: How should the output be structured (e.g., bullet points, table, essay)?
4. **Tone**: Should it be professional, casual, persuasive, or objective?

## The Anatomy of a Perfect Prompt

To consistently generate high-quality outputs, use the **CTFT** framework:
- **Context**: "I am a digital marketing manager for a SaaS startup."
- **Task**: "Write a 500-word email newsletter about our new AI-powered analytics feature."
- **Format**: "Use short paragraphs, include 3 bullet points highlighting benefits, and end with a clear Call to Action (CTA)."
- **Tone**: "Professional yet conversational and enthusiastic."

When you combine these elements, the AI understands exactly what you need.
