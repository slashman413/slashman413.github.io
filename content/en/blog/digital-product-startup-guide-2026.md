## What Are Digital Products and Why Are They So Important in 2026?

Digital products are intangible assets that can be distributed, downloaded, or accessed online. Common digital products include eBooks, online courses, templates (such as Notion or Feishu), software, design assets, and AI prompt libraries.

Compared to physical products, digital products have incredibly high profit margins and follow the principle of "create once, sell forever." This is exactly why more and more creators are flocking to this space.
