# 20 High-Performing Prompts for AI

*Free toolkit from [Slashman Tools](https://slashmantools.us/) — practical AI prompts, automation guides, and digital product tips.*

---

These 20 prompts cover the highest-value daily use cases: writing, coding, analysis, marketing, and design. Copy any prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or your favorite AI assistant, and replace the `[bracketed]` placeholders with your own context. The best results come from iterating — treat the first answer as a draft and refine.

---

## 1. Marketing Copywriter
"Act as an expert copywriter. Write a high-converting landing page for a B2B SaaS product that does X. Focus on the benefits, use the PAS (Problem-Agitation-Solution) framework, and include strong CTAs."

## 2. Technical Explainer
"Explain the concept of [Complex Topic] to a 10-year-old. Use analogies related to space or cars."

## 3. SEO Optimizer
"Provide a list of 10 long-tail keywords related to 'AI Automation'. For each keyword, give a suggested blog post title and a brief outline."

## 4. Code Reviewer
"Act as a Senior Developer. Review the following code for readability, performance, and security. Provide actionable suggestions for improvement: [Insert Code]"

## 5. Sales Email Sequence
"Write a 3-part cold email sequence targeting CTOs of mid-sized e-commerce companies. The goal is to get them on a 15-minute discovery call for our new AI-powered inventory tool."

## 6. Interview Preparation
"Act as a hiring manager for a Senior React Developer role. Ask me 5 technical questions one by one, waiting for my response before moving to the next. Grade my answers out of 10."

## 7. Product Manager
"Write a PRD (Product Requirements Document) for a new mobile app feature that allows users to scan receipts for expense tracking. Include user stories, edge cases, and success metrics."

## 8. Strategy Consultant
"Use the SWOT analysis framework to evaluate the current market position of [Company Name]. Suggest 3 strategic moves they should make in the next year."

## 9. Content Calendar
"Create a 30-day social media content calendar for a freelance graphic designer. Include post themes, formats (carousel, reel, text), and specific hook ideas."

## 10. Data Analyst
"Act as a data analyst. I will provide a dataset of sales figures. I want you to identify trends, seasonal variations, and outliers. [Insert Data]"

## 11. Contract Summarizer
"Summarize the key points, obligations, and liabilities in this legal contract. Highlight any unusual clauses in bold. [Insert Contract Text]"

## 12. Newsletter Creator
"Write a weekly newsletter for an audience of indie hackers. Topic: The rise of micro-SaaS. Include a witty introduction, 3 main takeaways, and a sponsor shoutout."

## 13. Onboarding Email
"Write a warm and welcoming day-0 onboarding email for our new app users. Explain the top 3 features they should try first and include a link to our community discord."

## 14. Bug Fixer
"I am getting the following error message: [Insert Error]. Analyze the potential causes and provide step-by-step instructions on how to resolve it. Here is the relevant code: [Insert Code]"

## 15. Investor Pitch
"Draft a 1-minute elevator pitch for a startup that uses AI to optimize home energy consumption. Make it punchy and focus on the ROI for the consumer."

## 16. UX Researcher
"Design a user interview script to understand how small business owners currently manage their payroll. Include icebreakers, open-ended questions, and wrap-up."

## 17. Brand Voice Guideline
"Analyze the following 3 blog posts and define the brand voice, tone, and style. Create a 1-page guideline for future writers to follow. [Insert Text]"

## 18. Crisis Communication
"Write an apology email to our customers regarding a 4-hour server outage that occurred today. Be transparent about what happened, what we are doing to fix it, and offer a 10% discount on their next month's bill."

## 19. Competitor Analysis
"Compare our product [Product A] with our main competitor [Product B]. Highlight our unique selling propositions (USPs) and areas where we need to improve."

## 20. Midjourney Prompt Generator
"Act as a prompt engineer for Midjourney. Generate 5 highly detailed prompts for a futuristic cyberpunk cityscape at sunset. Include parameters for aspect ratio, lighting, and camera angle."
